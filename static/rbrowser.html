                    About this browser
                    
                

                
The Web Browser runs on the server, not on your device. Websites see the server's address, not yours. The portal has no function that records, logs or stores the addresses you open, what you type, or what is on the screen, and it keeps no history: the browser's profile lives in memory and is destroyed when the session ends. The portal does log that you started and ended a session, when, and how much data it moved; administrators can see that you have a session and whether it is active. This is not protection from the people who run this portal: its administrators have full access to the server and could observe a running browser there, including sites you are signed in to. The picture of the browser travels through the portal process and through Cloudflare's network like every other page of the portal (encrypted in transit, readable at those two points). Name lookups go to a DNS-over-HTTPS resolver (Cloudflare's 1.1.1.1), which sees the names of the sites opened from the server. Do not use it for anything you would not open on a computer that belongs to someone else.

                
What is worse than a browser on your own device

                
    
                    
  1. It is a video of a browser: latency on every key and pointer move; scrolling and video are choppy at the stream cap; text is JPEG-compressed unless "Sharp" is chosen.
    2. 
                    
  2. No sound. No downloads, no uploads, no printing, no camera or microphone, no extensions, no page fullscreen.
    3. 
                    
  3. Text goes in only as typed keys; pasted text and the text panel type at most 5000 characters at a time, where an administrator has allowed them. Text comes out only through the "Copy from the browser" button, as plain text, at most 262144 bytes (longer text is cut), where an administrator has allowed it. The clipboard of your device and the clipboard in the remote browser are not linked. No password manager; nothing is remembered after the session.
    4. 
                    
  4. Sites see the server's address and location, a fresh profile every time (more CAPTCHAs, sign-in on every visit, some sites block data-centre addresses).
    5. 
                    
  5. Limited seats (2 by default), ends after 15 idle minutes, 4 hours, a portal restart or update, or when the traffic budget is used up.
    6. 
                    
  6. Keyboard shortcuts that your own browser or system takes (Ctrl+W, Ctrl+T, Ctrl+Tab, Alt+Tab) do not reach the remote browser except in fullscreen with keyboard lock, where supported. Where text in is allowed, Ctrl+V is taken by this page on purpose: it types the pasted text.
    7. 
                    
  7. Input methods for Chinese, Japanese and Korean work only through the phone-keyboard path (composed text), not key by key.
    8. 
                    
  8. Dead keys do not compose; type the finished character.
    9. 
                

                
The remote browser takes the portal theme's colour and its light or dark mode when a session starts. A theme change applies to the next session. Pages decide their own colours.
