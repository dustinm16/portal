Push notifications

                        
A phone or browser notice that a direct message, a mention or a followed stream needs attention, sent only to members who have no Portal tab open. It never carries a message text. It travels through the browser maker's push service (Google, Mozilla, Apple or Microsoft), encrypted to the device.

                        
                        
Subscribed devices: 0 (a number only; the addresses are never shown)
